WorthNot: Privacy Policy & Terms

Last Updated: August 2026

1. Privacy & Data Safety

WorthNot is designed with a "Local First" architecture.

  • Your financial SMS and bank notifications are processed locally on your device.
  • We do NOT upload, store, or share your transaction data, messages, or personal information to any external servers or third parties.
  • The app requires Notification Access and SMS permissions solely to automate spending detection for your own reflection.

2. Data Retention & Deletion

Because your data is stored locally on your device and not on our servers:

  • Data Retention: We do not retain any of your personal or financial data on external servers. All data remains on your device for as long as the app is installed.
  • Data Deletion: You can delete all your data at any time by simply uninstalling the App or clearing the App's storage in your device settings. This will permanently remove all locally stored transaction history and preferences.

3. Disclaimer

WorthNot is a spending awareness and reflection tool. It does not provide financial advice. The "alternative value" calculations are for educational and motivational purposes only. We are not responsible for any financial decisions made based on the app's data. Always consult a professional for financial planning.

4. Terms and Conditions

By using WorthNot, you agree that the app processes SMS and notifications locally on your device. You are responsible for ensuring that you have the right to allow the app to access this information. The app is provided "as is" without warranty of any kind.
